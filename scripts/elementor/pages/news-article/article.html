Singapore, 27 October 2026 – Building on its global expertise in fresh produce, food and agriculture, Messe Berlin today announced the launch of Fresh Food Expo Asia Pacific (FFE APAC), a new international B2B trade show bringing together the broader fresh food value chain across Asia Pacific.

The inaugural FFE APAC will take place from 16–18 November 2027 at Sands Expo & Convention Centre, Singapore. The event will bring together the industry across three core sectors: Fresh Food, Agri & Fresh Technology, and Logistics & Distribution.

FFE APAC builds on the international networks and industry expertise Messe Berlin has developed through events including FRUIT LOGISTICA in Berlin, the world’s leading trade show for the global fresh produce business, and Grüne Woche (Green Week), one of the world’s most established events for food, agriculture and horticulture.

The launch was marked by a ceremony between Messe Berlin and the Singapore Tourism Board (STB), reflecting their continued cooperation in bringing international business events to Singapore. Fresh Food Expo Asia Pacific will be supported by the Singapore Food Agency (SFA), Temasek and Enterprise Singapore (EnterpriseSG) and will form part of Singapore International Agri-Food Week (SIAW) from 2027, following Messe Berlin’s formal inclusion in the SIAW Memorandum of Understanding (MOU).

A new international trade show for the fresh food value chain

Asia Pacific’s food industry is undergoing significant transformation, driven by food security priorities, changing consumer demand, technological innovation, sustainability requirements and the need for more resilient supply chains.

FFE APAC will address these developments by providing a dedicated international business platform connecting producers, suppliers, buyers, distributors, retailers, foodservice companies, technology providers, logistics specialists, investors and other industry stakeholders from across Asia Pacific and around the world.

The trade show will focus on three core sectors:

  • Fresh Food – products spanning fresh produce, meat and poultry, seafood, dairy and more.
  • Agri & Fresh Technology – technologies and innovations spanning on-farm production, cold chain and ripening, freshness and shelf life, and processing and packaging.
  • Logistics & Distribution – services and solutions spanning air, land and sea freight, customs clearance, ports and warehousing, cold chain management, supply chain tracking, wholesale markets and distribution.

Through its exhibition, business matching, industry content and international network, FFE APAC will connect companies with buyers, partners and business opportunities across the region.

Singapore as a gateway to Asia Pacific’s fresh food industry

Singapore’s position as an international business, trade and logistics hub, combined with its connectivity across Asia Pacific and its focus on food security, innovation and international partnerships, provides a strong foundation for FFE APAC as an international trade show serving the fresh food industry across Asia Pacific.

FFE APAC will bring together industry, government, investors, innovators and international stakeholders, creating opportunities for business collaboration, trade, investment and innovation across the fresh food value chain.

Building on Messe Berlin’s Global Food Portfolio

Fresh Food Expo is a new international Messe Berlin brand created to address the broader fresh food value chain, with FFE APAC as its Asia Pacific edition.

The new brand complements Messe Berlin’s existing Global Food Portfolio. This includes FRUIT LOGISTICA in Berlin, the global meeting place for the fresh produce business; ASIA FRUIT LOGISTICA in Hong Kong, Asia’s leading international trade show for the fresh produce business; and the new FRUIT LOGISTICA Connect series, which brings the FRUIT LOGISTICA network and expertise into selected international markets, including FRUIT LOGISTICA Connect India in Mumbai from 2028. The portfolio also draws on Messe Berlin’s century of experience through Grüne Woche (Green Week) in Berlin, one of the world’s most established events for food, agriculture and horticulture.

While FRUIT LOGISTICA remains focused on the global fresh produce business, Fresh Food Expo expands into additional fresh food categories and the technologies, infrastructure and services supporting their production, distribution and trade.

FFE APAC will apply this broader concept to the specific market needs and opportunities of the Asia Pacific region, with the ambition of establishing a leading international trade show connecting the region’s fresh food industry with global markets.

About Messe Berlin

Messe Berlin has been a trade fair venue for over 200 years and has been one of the world’s most important for many decades. As a state-owned trade fair organisation, Messe Berlin designs, markets and organises hundreds of live events every year. Its aim is to be an outstanding host to visitors at all events, to provide the best possible business opportunities and to ensure fair conditions for everyone. This ethos is reflected in the company’s motto: Messe Berlin – Hosting the World.
