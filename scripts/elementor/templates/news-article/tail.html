About Fresh Food Expo Asia Pacific

Fresh Food Expo Asia Pacific (FFE APAC) is Messe Berlin’s international B2B trade show bringing together the broader fresh food value chain across Asia Pacific. The trade show focuses on three core sectors: Fresh Food, Agri & Fresh Technology, and Logistics & Distribution. FFE APAC connects international producers, suppliers, buyers, technology providers and logistics specialists, creating new business opportunities and partnerships across the region.

About Messe Berlin

Messe Berlin has been a trade fair venue for over 200 years and has been one of the world’s most important for many decades. As a state-owned trade fair organisation, Messe Berlin designs, markets and organises hundreds of live events every year. Its aim is to be an outstanding host to visitors at all events, to provide the best possible business opportunities and to ensure fair conditions for everyone. This ethos is reflected in the company’s motto: Messe Berlin – Hosting the World.
